WeatherGlass — main panel mocks

Selected direction: A · Sky-first. B–D remain below as reference; new iterations land on A.

A · Sky-first — selected direction. Today's structure, tightened: temperature hero on open sky, the week strip directly underneath it, then the full-bleed chart and stats. Picking a day re-skins the whole sky to that day’s weather, like the extension. Least change from the extension; the sky stays the product.

B · Modular grid. Everything is a card on a dimmed sky: hero card, sun/moon card, wide chart card, stat cards, day cards. Reads well with the sky off or in light mode, and widgets can be rearranged later — at the cost of the open-sky feel.

D · Rail + cities (C, combined). No separate sidebar: the rail opens on your default city, the chips along its top switch cities (try them — hero, location and sky follow), and + adds one. The 8-day list stays under the hero; the data side is untouched. Frees ~260px of window width versus the two-column layout.

C · Split rail (dense). The sky is confined to a left rail holding the hero and a vertical day list; the right side is a flat, data-dense surface with a stats table. Best at large window sizes and for scanning many days; least atmospheric.
